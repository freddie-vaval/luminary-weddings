# Luminary Weddings — Working Group Decision Pack

**Prepared:** 18 July 2026  
**Purpose:** Decide whether Luminary Weddings is viable, agree the revenue model, and authorise a tightly controlled 90-day pilot.

## 1. Recommended decision

**Proceed to a 90-day concierge pilot, not a full marketplace launch.**

> **Working-group commercial update:** The independent commercial review recommended a stronger hybrid test than the planner-only model below: charge the couple a **£295 refundable curation fee** after qualification, plus a **10% planner success fee capped at £2,000**. Founding planners would pay **7.5% capped at £1,250 for their first placement**. This should be discussed alongside the 15% planner-only option before any price is published. The group was unanimous that the historical uncapped 25% model should not launch.

Luminary has a credible wedge if it is positioned as **private, human planner matchmaking for high-consideration UK and destination weddings**. It is not viable as another broad directory: Bridebook and Hitched already compete at directory scale. The first product should therefore be a managed service supported by simple software, not a large platform.

### Why it may work

- There is a large underlying market: the ONS recorded **231,949 marriages and civil partnerships in England and Wales in 2023**.
- Couples face a high-trust, high-value buying decision and broad directories create choice overload.
- Established planners value qualified introductions but do not want low-intent leads or another listing bill.
- Luminary can start manually: planner intake, couple brief, shortlist, introduction, attribution and commission tracking.
- The brand can specialise in planner fit and destination competence rather than supplier breadth.

### Why it may fail

- A new brand has no trusted planner supply, proven matches or couple acquisition engine.
- A **25% commission may be too expensive** for good planners unless the lead is exclusive, genuinely qualified and converts reliably.
- Paid advertising can make the economics poor before conversion rates are known.
- Unverified planner profiles or testimonials would damage trust immediately.
- The current domain does not expose the repository website: it resolves through Cloudflare to a Pixieset response, while automated checks receive HTTP 403.

## 2. The proposition to test

### For couples

> Tell us the wedding you want to create. Luminary privately introduces two or three planners selected around your vision, destination, scale, budget and availability.

- Personal review rather than automated matching.
- A small shortlist rather than a public directory.
- No charge to couples from Luminary.
- Direct contracting and payment between couple and planner.

### For planners

> A selective source of well-matched, attributable enquiries, with no subscription and payment only when an introduced couple becomes a confirmed client.

The founding planner offer should not promise a volume of leads. It should promise careful qualification, clear attribution, commercial transparency and the right to decline every introduction.

## 3. Revenue model — decision required

### Existing model recovered from the repository

- **25% of the planner's confirmed planning fee**.
- Paid by the planner only after a confirmed booking.
- No upfront charge or monthly subscription.
- The couple pays the planner directly.

At a £12,000 planning fee:

| Success fee | Luminary earns | Planner retains |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 10% | £1,200 | £10,800 |
| 15% | £1,800 | £10,200 |
| 20% | £2,400 | £9,600 |
| **25% current proposal** | **£3,000** | **£9,000** |

### Recommended pilot model

**Founding pilot: 15% success fee, capped at £3,000 per confirmed booking for the first 10 placements.**

- No joining fee.
- No subscription.
- Calculated on the planner's professional planning fee **excluding VAT, travel and third-party/pass-through supplier costs**.
- Earned only after the planner receives the couple's non-refundable booking payment.
- Collected in proportion to payments received, or as one agreed invoice after the booking payment clears.
- Applies to an introduced couple who books within a clearly defined attribution window, proposed at 12 months.
- Refund/credit treatment follows the planner's actual client refund under agreed cancellation rules.

### Why not lock in 25% yet

A 25% share is attractive to Luminary but asks a planner to surrender £3,000 on a £12,000 fee. That may work for a truly incremental, exclusive, high-intent client; it is difficult to justify before Luminary proves lead quality and conversion. UKAWP's public ethics guidance also shows how sensitive the industry is to undisclosed commissions and kickbacks. Full transparency to planners and couples is therefore non-negotiable.

### Revenue-model options for the group

1. **15% founding success fee** — easiest planner adoption; recommended for the pilot.
2. **20% standard success fee after proof** — viable once conversion and service levels are established.
3. **25% premium/exclusive introduction** — only if Luminary performs substantial qualification, exclusivity and close support.
4. **Hybrid later** — lower 10–15% success fee plus an optional paid visibility/content service; do not introduce this during the pilot.

### Commercial clauses to agree

- What counts as a Luminary introduction.
- Attribution window and duplicate/pre-existing contacts.
- Exact fee base and VAT treatment.
- When commission is earned, invoiced and due.
- Instalments, cancellations, refunds and chargebacks.
- Planner reporting and reasonable booking verification.
- Non-circumvention without preventing genuine pre-existing relationships.
- Whether Luminary discloses that it receives a planner-paid success fee to couples; recommendation: **yes, plainly**.
- Whether planners can price Luminary couples differently; recommendation: **no**.

## 5. Ninety-minute working group agenda

### 0–10 minutes — outcome and guardrails

- Are we testing a managed matchmaking service or building a marketplace?
- Agree: no fake profiles, testimonials, venues, demand or credentials.
- Agree the exact couple and planner segment for the pilot.

### 10–25 minutes — customer problem and wedge

- What fails when couples search directories or contact planners directly?
- Which couples need Luminary enough to act now?
- Why would a strong planner accept an introduction from Luminary?
- Is the initial wedge UK luxury, UK-to-Europe destination, or one narrower corridor?

### 25–45 minutes — revenue model

- Discuss the 15%, 20% and 25% options using the £12,000 example.
- Agree fee base, cap, VAT, payment trigger, attribution and cancellations.
- Decide what couples are told about planner-paid commission.
- Decide whether founding terms expire after 10 placements or 12 months.
- Agree the minimum gross contribution required per booking.

### 45–60 minutes — manual service workflow

- Minimum information needed from couples.
- Planner admission criteria and evidence.
- Who reviews briefs and within what response time.
- How planner availability is checked before an introduction.
- How consent is obtained before sharing personal data.
- How introductions, outcomes and commissions are tracked.
- Complaint, mismatch and cancellation handling.

### 60–75 minutes — supply and demand acquisition

- Which first 20 planners are credible targets and why?
- Which five destination/venue relationships can create authority?
- First three acquisition channels: founder outreach, partner referrals and high-intent search content.
- What evidence is required before paid advertising.
- Which real planner imagery, case studies and permissions can be used.

### 75–90 minutes — pilot commitment

- Approve or reject the pilot revenue model.
- Name owners for planner supply, couple acquisition, operations, legal and website.
- Approve pilot targets and stop conditions.
- Set weekly review and day-30/day-60/day-90 decisions.

## 6. Planner admission criteria

A planner should not be presented as part of the network until Luminary has checked:

- Trading identity, website and direct contact details.
- Portfolio ownership and permission to display images.
- Public liability and professional indemnity insurance appropriate to their work.
- References or verifiable recent client work.
- Core destinations and first-hand destination experience.
- Typical planning fee and minimum engagement.
- Capacity and response-time commitment.
- Contracting, deposit and cancellation process.
- Agreement to data, attribution and commission terms.

## 7. Manual pilot workflow

1. Planner applies or is invited.
2. Luminary checks evidence and agrees founding terms.
3. Couple submits a structured brief and consents to data sharing.
4. Luminary qualifies budget, date, destination, scope and readiness.
5. Luminary checks availability privately with suitable planners.
6. Couple receives two or three contextual introductions.
7. Planner accepts or declines the introduction.
8. Luminary records consultation, proposal and outcome.
9. Confirmed booking triggers success-fee tracking.
10. Couple and planner feedback improves future matching.

Do not build automated matching, planner dashboards, Stripe Connect or a mobile app before this manual loop produces repeated bookings.

## 8. Pilot targets and kill criteria

### Day 30

- 10 planner conversations.
- At least 5 planners willing to sign the proposed founding terms.
- 5 real couple discovery interviews.
- One agreed segment and destination wedge.
- Website enquiry and planner-interest journeys ready for controlled traffic.

### Day 60

- 8 vetted founding planners.
- 10 qualified couple enquiries from attributable sources.
- At least 5 accepted planner introductions.
- Median first response under one working day.

### Day 90

- 20 qualified couple enquiries.
- 10 accepted introductions.
- At least 2 confirmed paid planner bookings.
- Positive contribution after direct acquisition and handling costs.
- At least 70% of planners say the introduction quality justified the proposed fee.

### Pause or change direction if

- Fewer than 5 credible planners accept even the 15% founding model.
- Enquiries are mostly below planner minimum fees.
- No booking occurs after 10 properly qualified introductions.
- Paid acquisition cost consumes the likely success fee.
- Luminary cannot verify portfolios, consent, attribution or booking outcomes reliably.

## 10. Pre-mortem

Assume Luminary failed after six months:

| Failure | Early warning | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Good planners reject the economics | Objections focus on 25% fee | Pilot at 15%, cap it, prove conversion before increasing |
| Couples do not trust a new intermediary | Enquiries abandon before sharing budget | Use named humans, real evidence, transparent process and private shortlists |
| Marketing attracts low-budget demand | Most enquiries miss planner minimums | State positioning clearly and qualify budget early |
| Attribution disputes destroy relationships | Planners report pre-existing contacts | Timestamp introductions and define duplicates/attribution contractually |
| Paid ads consume all revenue | CAC approaches likely commission | Founder/partner/SEO channels first; cap experiments |
| Website overstates reality | Visitors see invented proof | Publish only verified planners, testimonials and case studies |
| Operations cannot respond quickly | Briefs wait over one working day | Small launch volume, named operator and SLA dashboard |

## 11. Independent working-group counterproposal

The commercial reviewer pressure-tested planner willingness against Bark, Bridebook and venue/network referrals and recommended testing a **buyer-paid concierge plus lower planner commission**:

- Couple pays **£295 after qualification** and before curation begins.
- Refund the £295 if Luminary cannot provide at least two suitable, available planners within five working days.
- Planner pays **10% of professional planning/design fees**, excluding VAT and pass-through costs.
- Planner success fee: **£750 minimum, £2,000 maximum**.
- Founding planner's first placement: **7.5%, capped at £1,250**.
- Planner approves each brief before identity/contact details are shared.
- No blanket exclusivity and no higher quote for Luminary couples.

The buyer fee funds unsuccessful matching work and tests whether couples truly value independent curation. The group should decide between this hybrid model and the simpler 15% planner-only pilot; **the uncapped 25% model should not launch**.

### Stronger 90-day validation targets

- Interview 30 established planners; sign 10 and keep at least six active.
- At least 70% of qualified planners accept the capped 10% standard model.
- Generate 60 qualified £75,000+ wedding enquiries.
- Convert at least 35% of qualified enquiries into £295 paid briefs.
- Provide two accepted planner options within five working days for at least 80% of paid briefs.
- Achieve three confirmed placements, or two placements plus three final-stage proposals.
- Keep acquisition cost per paid brief at or below £150.
- Keep concierge effort at or below 2.5 hours per paid brief.
- Reach at least £750 contribution per confirmed booking.
